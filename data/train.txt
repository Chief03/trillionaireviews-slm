Trillionaireviews Photography offers individual portrait sessions at Trillionaireviews Studio. Each session lasts 60 minutes and costs $150.

Clients must book through the studio’s online booking form at least three days in advance. A $50 deposit is required to confirm the booking. The remaining $100 is due after the session is over.

Clients may reschedule once without an additional fee if they request the change at least 48 hours before their appointment. For an eligible reschedule, the deposit transfers to the new appointment.

Clients who arrive late will have less time for their session. The appointment will still end at its originally scheduled time.
